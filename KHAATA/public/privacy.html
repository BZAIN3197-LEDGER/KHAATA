Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what happens when you use KHAATA, an AI-assisted budgeting tool hosted at khaata-lemon.vercel.app.

Information you enter

When you generate a budget, the site sends the country, city, budget dates, available budget amount, household counts and any optional budgeting note you submit to its server so it can create a response. Do not enter passwords, bank or payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information in the suggestion field.

AI processing and economic data

Budget requests are processed using Groq's AI service. The site also requests country-level economic indicators from the World Bank API. Those requests are used to produce the response and show its data sources. These third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy practices; review their policies before using the service.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted through Vercel. Hosting providers may process technical information such as request metadata and security logs to deliver and protect the service. This page does not promise that server or provider logs are never retained.

Advertising and cookies

KHAATA loads Google AdSense advertising code. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. Other vendors or ad networks may also use cookies as permitted by their own policies and applicable rules.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can learn more in Google's advertising information or visit YourAdChoices for information about interest-based advertising.

How to limit information shared

You can choose not to use the budget generator and should avoid submitting information that identifies you or another person. You can use your browser settings to control cookies, although some third-party features may behave differently.

Children and privacy

The service is a general budgeting tool, not a place to submit private information about children or other family members. Provide household counts rather than names or personal details.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision published here.

Contact

No dedicated contact address is currently published on this website. Do not assume this page creates a support channel.
