Frequently Asked Questions

What is Khaata?

Khaata is an AI-powered budgeting tool that generates personalized budget estimates based on information provided by the user.

How does Khaata create a budget?

Khaata considers information such as location, available money, budget period, household size and user-provided preferences when generating a budget.

Does Khaata provide financial advice?

Khaata provides budgeting estimates and educational information. Its results should not be treated as professional financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Why does Khaata ask for my city?

Living costs can vary between cities. The selected city provides additional geographical context when generating the budget.

Can I use Khaata for a family?

Yes. Khaata allows users to specify household members across several age groups so household size can be considered when generating a budget.

Are Khaata's budget numbers exact?

No. They are estimates. Actual costs can vary according to location, household circumstances, lifestyle and changing prices.

Can I add special requirements?

Yes. The suggestion box allows users to provide additional information about their goals, preferences or circumstances.

Does Khaata use live prices?

Khaata's current system does not claim to retrieve live local prices. Generated amounts are estimates based on the information available to the AI.
