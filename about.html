About Khaata

Khaata is an AI-powered budgeting tool designed to help individuals and families organize their available money and understand how it could be distributed across different spending categories.

Khaata considers information such as location, budget period, available money and household size when generating a personalized budget.

What Khaata Does

  • Creates personalized budget allocations.
  • Considers household size and age groups.
  • Uses location as part of the budgeting context.
  • Provides categories such as food, housing, transportation, utilities and education.
  • Provides explanations and recommendations alongside the generated budget.

How Khaata Should Be Used

A generated budget is a starting point rather than a guaranteed description of someone's actual expenses. Costs can vary significantly between households, cities and countries.

Users should review the generated allocation and adjust it according to their actual expenses, priorities and financial circumstances.

Important Information

Khaata provides budgeting estimates and educational information. It is not professional financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
