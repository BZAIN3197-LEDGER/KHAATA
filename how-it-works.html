How Khaata Works

Khaata creates a personalized budget using information provided by the user. The goal is to provide a useful starting point for understanding and organizing expenses.

1. Select your country

Choose the country where the budget will be used. This provides geographical context for the budget.

2. Select your city

Living costs can differ between cities, so Khaata uses the selected city as part of its budgeting context.

3. Enter your budget period

Select the start and end dates for the period you want to plan.

4. Enter your available money

Enter the amount of money available for the selected budget period.

5. Add household information

Enter the number of infants, toddlers, children, teenagers, adults and older adults in the household.

6. Add your preferences

The suggestion box allows you to provide additional information such as savings goals, spending priorities or unusual circumstances.

7. Generate your budget

Khaata's AI analyzes the information and generates percentage allocations for different spending categories.

What the Results Include

  • Total budget allocation
  • Category percentages
  • Estimated spending amounts
  • Economic information
  • Potential trade-offs
  • A personalized budgeting insight

Why Results Can Differ

There is no single budget that works for every household. Housing costs, food prices, transportation, education, healthcare and household size can all affect spending.

Khaata's results should therefore be reviewed and adjusted according to actual circumstances.
